# ps-builder-skills

A collection of skills that turn AI agents into Process Street builders. These skills enable agents to:

- **Create** pages and workflows in Process Street from source documents (input)
- **Export** pages and workflows out of Process Street into other formats (output)

Skills are organized by the format of the source material (where business policies, SOPs, and processes are documented) and by the target export format.

## Available skills

Skills are grouped by input/output format:

- **Documents** — create workflows from (or export to) PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, and CSV files
- **BPMN and images** — create workflows from BPMN diagrams or process images
- **Meeting transcripts and recordings** — create workflows from meeting notes, transcripts, or call recordings
- **Videos** — create workflows from screen recordings or video walkthroughs of a process (e.g. Loom, Zoom recordings, Scribe, MP4 screencasts, YouTube tutorials)
